# UK Endorsing Bodies 2026: Official Assessment Criteria & Guide

This repository provides an authoritative technical breakdown of the authorized assessing bodies approved by the UK Home Office to issue Letters of Endorsement for the **UK Innovator Founder Visa**.

---

## 🏛️ Official UK Endorsing Bodies (2026)
Applicants seeking business immigration to the UK must secure formal endorsement from one of the official contracted assessing organizations:
1. **Envestors Limited:** Specializes in scalable early-stage technology enterprises and angel investor networks.
2. **UK Endorsement Services (UKES):** Cross-sector business model validation with focus on UK job creation.
3. **Innovator International:** Global founder support and international expansion scaling.

---

## 🎯 Statutory Endorsement Assessment Pillars
Every business proposal is evaluated against three core statutory requirements:
- **Innovation:** The business proposition must offer an original, market-tested solution that introduces new competition to the UK market.
- **Viability:** Sound financial runway, founder capability, and operational cash-flow projections.
- **Scalability:** Realistic potential for nationwide job creation and economic growth in the United Kingdom.
